Week ahead

November 23 to 27, 2026

Stocks swung hard this week. After an 8.6% drop last week, the S&P 500 jumped 4.7% Monday to 7,237, its second biggest point gain ever, as Nvidia rose about 31% on news about its leadership and the Nasdaq gained 6.5%. Tuesday was mixed. Consumer confidence jumped to 103.3, far above forecasts, and the Dow closed above 50,000 for the first time, but the S&P 500 slipped 0.5% and the Nasdaq fell 1.5% as tech cooled. Dell crushed estimates after the bell and raised its outlook, and most big reports this week beat, though Deere missed Wednesday morning. Wednesday's data ran hot. Core PCE rose 3.3% from a year ago versus 3.2% expected, third quarter growth was revised up to 4.3%, claims fell to 189,000 and core capital goods orders jumped 1.2%. That backs a December 9 hike. Still ahead today are new home sales, a 7 year auction and the Beige Book. US markets are closed Thursday, and Friday stocks close at 1:00 PM ET and bonds at 2:00 PM ET.

ConsensusNonePriorNone

The only scheduled Fed speaker found for the week, and a permanent voter. The Fed goes quiet from midnight Saturday ahead of the December meeting. He recently said more hikes will likely be needed.
