Week ahead

November 16 to 20, 2026

The rate hike story is now nearly settled. October CPI ran hot at 0.4% headline and 0.3% core, and while PPI came in a bit cooler than feared, CME FedWatch prices a December hike at close to 100%, with roughly 90% odds of a quarter point and 10% of a half point. This week shifts the spotlight to AI and the consumer. Nvidia reports Tuesday after the close, with the stock down about 20% so far in November and Colette Kress serving as interim CEO. October retail sales land the same morning, Home Depot, Lowe's, Target, TJX and Walmart report through the week, and the FOMC minutes on Wednesday will show how many officials wanted to hike in October. Chair Warsh opens the week with remarks Monday at noon.

Biggest day of the week

Tuesday Nov 17: retail sales and Home Depot in the morning, Nvidia after the close

Tuesday packs the consumer and the AI trade into one session. Retail sales at 8:30 AM ET show whether shoppers are holding up under rising rates, and Home Depot reports before the open. After the bell, Nvidia reports with consensus near $109B in revenue and $2.47 in EPS, the first report with an interim CEO and with the stock down about 20% this month. Its data center guidance will set the tone for chips and AI infrastructure into year end.
