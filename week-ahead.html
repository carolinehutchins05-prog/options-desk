Week ahead

November 16 to 20, 2026

The first half of the week brought a selloff, a beat and a mixed picture. Stocks fell Monday and Tuesday, with the S&P 500 sliding about 1% Tuesday to a five month low as tech, chips and software led and all 11 sectors finished lower. October retail sales rose 0.2%, a touch above forecasts, while industrial production fell 0.4% on a utilities drop and the Empire State survey sank to 7.8. Chair Warsh spoke about AI and productivity but gave no rate signal, so a December hike stays priced. Nvidia then beat with $115.8B in revenue and guided to $130B, sending Nasdaq futures up almost 1% Wednesday morning. Target and Lowe's beat, though both traded lower early. Still ahead: the FOMC minutes at 2:00 PM today, Palo Alto tonight, jobless claims, the Philly Fed, Walmart and the Anthropic IPO debut Thursday, and Intuit after Thursday's close.

2:00 PMHigh impact

FOMC minutes (Oct 27 to 28 meeting)

ConsensusNonePriorNone

Details of the meeting where the Fed held rates with two dissents for a hike. Watch how many officials saw a hike as likely soon and any debate on a larger move. Released today at 2:00 PM ET, with the 10 year yield near 5.1%.

How markets could react

Minutes showing broad support for hiking, or talk of more than one hike, would lift short yields and the dollar and hit tech. A divided or cautious tone would ease fears of a half point move and help growth stocks. A December hike is already priced, so the path beyond it matters most.
