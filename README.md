# Options Desk

Automated daily screen for single leg long calls and puts. Two runs each weekday:

- **Premarket plan**, 8:36 AM ET. Candidates and a provisional contract off the prior close.
- **Live verdict**, 10:11 AM ET. The same candidates regraded on live chains, 30 minutes after the open.

Every candidate must clear a fixed scorecard: delta 0.50 to 0.65, breakeven within 3 percent, spread under 5 percent, IV under 40 percent, theta under 5 percent of mark per day, open interest 1,000+, volume 500+, bid and ask size 20+ each, DTE 21 to 60, earnings clear of expiry.

Most days report no candidates. That is the expected result, not a failure.
