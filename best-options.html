How to read this

Return on a normal move. We take each stock's own recent volatility, scale it to one week, and estimate what the option would be worth if the stock moved that much in the contract's favor, minus the spread you pay going in and out. Options priced cheaply compared with how the stock really moves rank higher.

Open interest is how many contracts are open. Every contract here has at least 2,000 open and 100 traded today, so you can get in and out.

Earnings. Anything reporting inside the next eight days is removed, because the price drop after a report can erase the gain. If a report falls after that but before expiry it is flagged: sell before it.

Not investment advice. A personal research log. Estimates come from a model, not real fills. Free, delayed quotes can be stale, so check the live chain before any trade.
