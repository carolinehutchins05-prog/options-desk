## How to read this

Two files per weekday appear under [archive](archive/): a **premarket plan** built on the prior close, and a **live verdict** regraded on real quotes 30 minutes after the open.

The verdict is the one that counts. If a name gapped so far at the open that its breakeven moved beyond 3 percent, it is marked **gapped past entry** and dropped rather than chased with a further strike.

## The gates

| Gate | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Delta | 0.50 to 0.65 |
| Breakeven | within 3% of spot |
| Spread | 5% of mark or less |
| IV | under 40%, over 70 rejected |
| Theta | under 5% of mark per day |
| Open interest | 1,000 or more |
| Volume | 500 or more |
| Bid and ask size | 20 or more each |
| DTE | 21 to 60 |
| Earnings | clear of expiry |
