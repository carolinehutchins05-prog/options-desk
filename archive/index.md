# Daily log

<p class="lede">Two entries per weekday: a premarket plan built on the prior close, and a live verdict regraded on real quotes thirty minutes after the open. Both are published whether or not anything cleared.</p>

The gap between the two is the useful part. A candidate that looked tradable at 8:30 and was dropped at 10:00 for moving past its break even is the most common outcome, and seeing how often that happens is the point of keeping both.

No run published yet. The first automated entry posts on the next trading morning.

Entries carry no position, account, or sizing information. See [methodology]({{ '/methodology' | relative_url }}) for what is checked.
